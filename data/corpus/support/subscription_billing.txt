Section billing.subscription_cancel.
Subscription cancellation policy. Customers may cancel a subscription at any time from account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund the current billing period. Access remains active until the end of the paid period unless the product plan states otherwise.

中文要点：取消订阅只是不再续费，不等于自动退当前周期费用。通常会员权益会保留到已付款周期结束。

Section billing.renewal_refund.
Renewal refunds can be approved within 7 calendar days after renewal if there has been no meaningful usage after the renewal time. Meaningful usage includes opening premium content, exporting files, starting paid compute tasks, or using paid support credits. Refunds after 7 days require manager approval.

中文要点：用户问“续费后没用能不能退款”时，续费后 7 个自然日内且没有明显使用，可以批准退款。超过 7 天需要主管审批。

Section billing.invoice_tax.
Invoices are available in account billing history after payment succeeds. Tax invoice requests must include legal billing name, tax identification number if applicable, billing address, order number, and recipient email. Support should not edit paid invoices unless finance approves the correction.

中文要点：发票在付款成功后可下载。税务发票需要抬头、税号、地址、订单号和接收邮箱。已付款发票修改需要财务批准。
