Section returns.window.
Support returns and refunds policy. Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. The item must be unused, complete, and returned with its original accessories and packaging. Return requests after 30 days are normally declined unless a documented carrier delay or confirmed product defect prevented timely use.

中文要点：用户问“退款多久到账”“怎么退货”“退货窗口多久”时，本条政策适用。退货窗口是签收后 30 个自然日。商品需要未使用、配件齐全，并保留原包装。

Section returns.refund_timing.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the warehouse receives and inspects the returned item. Standard refund processing takes up to 7 business days after inspection approval. Bank or card issuer posting can add another 2 to 5 business days. Store credit refunds are usually available within 24 hours after approval.

中文要点：退款不是用户寄出后马上到账，而是仓库验收通过后开始处理。原支付方式退款通常在验收通过后 7 个工作日内完成；银行入账可能再增加 2 到 5 个工作日。

Section returns.exclusions.
Final sale items, gift cards, personalized items, opened hygiene products, and digital goods after download or activation are not eligible for standard return. Digital goods can be refunded within 14 days only if they have not been downloaded, activated, or consumed. Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by a confirmed fulfillment error or product defect.

中文要点：特价清仓、礼品卡、个性化商品、已拆封卫生用品，以及已下载或已激活的数字商品通常不能退。若是发错货或商品缺陷，运费可以退。
