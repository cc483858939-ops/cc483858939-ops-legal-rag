Section warranty.coverage.
Hardware warranty policy. Covered devices include a 12-month limited warranty starting from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, lost accessories, unauthorized repair, or damage caused by unsupported chargers.

中文要点：用户问“保修多久”“进水保不保”时，本条适用。保修期是签收日起 12 个月。制造缺陷和正常使用下硬件故障可保；进水、摔坏、私拆、外观磨损和非支持充电器损坏不保。

Section warranty.rma.
Return merchandise authorization requires proof of purchase, device serial number, fault description, troubleshooting steps already completed, and photos or diagnostic logs when available. Support should create the RMA within 5 business days after receiving complete information. Replacement is approved only after inspection confirms a covered defect.

中文要点：RMA 需要购买凭证、序列号、故障描述、已完成排查步骤，以及照片或诊断日志。材料齐全后 5 个工作日内创建 RMA。

Section warranty.repair_status.
Repair status updates are sent when the device is received, inspected, repaired, replaced, or shipped back. Standard repair turnaround is 10 to 15 business days after the repair center receives the device. Delays must be escalated if no status update is available for more than 7 business days.

中文要点：维修中心收到设备后，标准维修周期是 10 到 15 个工作日。超过 7 个工作日没有状态更新应升级跟进。
