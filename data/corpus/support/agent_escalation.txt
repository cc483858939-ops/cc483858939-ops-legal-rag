Section escalation.live_agent.
Support escalation policy. The assistant or first-line agent should escalate to a live specialist when the customer has a payment failure after two retry attempts, an inaccessible account after password reset, a security or suspected takeover issue, a legal or law-enforcement request, a chargeback, a safety complaint, or a customer explicitly asks for a human after self-service steps fail.

中文要点：用户问“什么时候转人工”时，本条适用。支付两次失败、重置后仍无法登录、疑似盗号、法律或执法请求、拒付、安全投诉、自助失败后明确要求人工，都应转人工或专员。

Section escalation.priority.
High priority support queues include account security, payment risk, active subscription outage, shipment lost with time-sensitive replacement, and accessibility blockers. Normal refund status questions, invoice download guidance, and shipping ETA questions should remain in standard support unless the customer meets a priority condition.

中文要点：高优先级包括账户安全、支付风控、订阅服务中断、紧急补发、无障碍阻塞。普通退款进度、发票下载和物流预计到达时间一般不需要高优先级。

Section escalation.notes.
Escalation notes must include customer identity verification status, order or account identifiers, issue summary, troubleshooting already completed, customer impact, requested outcome, and any relevant screenshots or logs. Missing escalation notes slow down resolution and may cause reassignment back to first-line support.

中文要点：升级工单必须写清身份验证状态、订单或账号标识、问题摘要、已排查步骤、用户影响、期望结果、截图或日志。
