Section order.cancel.
Order cancellation policy. A customer can cancel an order while its status is pending payment, paid, or awaiting fulfillment. Once the fulfillment label is printed, cancellation is no longer guaranteed. If the package has already shipped, support should use the return process after delivery instead of promising cancellation.

中文要点：用户问“订单能不能取消”时，先看订单状态。未发货前通常可以取消；物流面单已生成后不保证能取消；已经发货后走收货后的退货流程。

Section order.address_change.
Address changes are allowed within 1 hour after order placement or before fulfillment starts, whichever is earlier. Support must verify the customer identity and confirm the full replacement address. Address changes are not allowed after shipment. After shipment, the customer may request carrier redirect if the carrier supports it.

中文要点：用户问“发货后能不能改地址”时，答案是发货后不能由客服直接改地址，只能看承运商是否支持改派。下单后 1 小时内或履约前可以尝试修改地址。

Section order.items.
Item quantity, color, size, and bundle changes are allowed only before fulfillment begins. If the requested item is more expensive, the original order should be canceled and a new order should be placed. If the requested item is cheaper, support should cancel and ask the customer to reorder instead of issuing partial manual refunds.

中文要点：改颜色、尺码、数量也必须在履约前处理。价格变化时优先取消重下单，不建议人工补差价。
